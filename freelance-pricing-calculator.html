Freelance Pricing Calculator

A freelance pricing calculator helps you estimate rates that support your income goals, business costs, and desired profit margin.

Introduction

Pricing is one of the most important decisions for freelancers because it directly affects cash flow, sustainability, and growth.

How It Works

You use your income target, expected expenses, and working hours to estimate a rate that makes sense for your business.

Calculation Explanation

Most pricing estimates combine required revenue, billable time, and profit needs to produce a practical rate for clients.

Examples

  • Estimate a rate for a target monthly income
  • Compare prices for different weekly schedules
  • Adjust pricing for overhead and tax obligations

FAQ

Why do freelancers need pricing guidance?

It helps you avoid undercharging and build a financially healthy business.

Can this help with client proposals?

Yes. Clear pricing makes it easier to discuss scope, value, and budget with clients.
