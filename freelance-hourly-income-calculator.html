Freelance Hourly Income Calculator

A freelance hourly income calculator helps you estimate how much you need to earn per hour to meet your personal and business goals.

Introduction

Freelancers often need to translate annual targets, monthly income goals, and business costs into a clear hourly benchmark.

How It Works

You combine your desired income, expenses, and working hours to estimate the hourly amount needed to sustain your freelance business.

Calculation Explanation

The estimate is usually based on total required income divided by billable hours, giving you a practical benchmark for pricing and planning.

Examples

  • Estimate hourly income for a target monthly revenue goal
  • Compare rates for different weekly work schedules
  • Plan hourly pricing around expenses and taxes

How hourly income is estimated

Use the rate clients pay, the hours you can actually invoice, and the number of working weeks in the period. Administrative time should not be counted as billable unless a client pays for it.

Hourly income formula

Estimated gross income = hourly rate x billable hours per week x working weeks. Net planning income requires separate deductions for business expenses and taxes.

Hourly earnings example

At $65 per hour, 24 billable hours per week, and 46 working weeks, estimated gross annual freelance income is $71,760 before expenses and taxes.
