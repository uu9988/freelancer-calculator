Freelance Hourly Rate Calculator

A freelance hourly rate calculator helps you estimate the rate you should charge for your time based on your business goals and costs.

Introduction

Choosing the right hourly rate is essential for freelancers who want to stay profitable and avoid undercharging for their skills.

How It Works

You combine your target income, business costs, and available billable hours to estimate the hourly rate needed for sustainability.

Calculation Explanation

The estimate is typically built from required revenue divided by billable hours, then adjusted for taxes and expenses.

Examples

  • Estimate a rate for a desired annual income
  • Compare rates for different weekly schedules
  • Adjust pricing for tax and overhead assumptions

FAQ

Why should I calculate my hourly rate?

It helps you set a realistic price for your work and protect your earning potential.

Can this guide my client pricing?

Yes. A clear rate estimate makes it easier to justify pricing to clients and build trust.
