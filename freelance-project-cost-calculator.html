Freelance Project Cost Calculator

Estimate the internal cost of delivering a freelance project from labor, subcontractors, other direct expenses, overhead, and a contingency allowance.

Use one currency for every monetary input. This is an internal delivery-cost estimate, not a client quote, invoice, profit calculation, or tax calculation.

Project delivery assumptions

Enter your expected internal effort and the costs required to complete this project. Use 0 for expense or percentage fields that do not apply.

All money inputs must use the same currency. The calculator performs no currency conversion and does not add profit or taxes.

Your delivery cost estimate

Enter your project assumptions and select Calculate Project Cost.

Internal labor cost
Internal project hours multiplied by the internal hourly cost.
Direct project expenses
Subcontractor costs plus other expenses that exist specifically because of this project.
Overhead allocation
A share of general business operating costs assigned to project delivery.
Contingency
A cost allowance for reasonable uncertainty, separate from profit.
Total estimated delivery cost
The internal amount estimated to complete the work before adding profit or creating a client quote.

How the project cost calculation works

The calculator separates internal labor from direct purchases and subcontractor payments, then allocates overhead and applies contingency. This avoids treating a client-facing price as though it were the cost of delivery.

Calculation formulas

Internal labor cost
estimated internal project hours × internal hourly cost

Direct project expenses
subcontractor costs + other direct project expenses

Base delivery cost
internal labor cost + direct project expenses

Overhead allocation amount
base delivery cost × overhead allocation percentage

Cost before contingency
base delivery cost + overhead allocation amount

Contingency amount
cost before contingency × contingency percentage

Total estimated delivery cost
cost before contingency + contingency amount

Average delivery cost per internal hour
total estimated delivery cost ÷ estimated internal project hours

Complete project cost example

These are example assumptions, not recommended defaults.

  • Estimated internal project hours: 40
  • Internal hourly cost: $35
  • Subcontractor costs: $600
  • Other direct project expenses: $250
  • Overhead allocation: 10%
  • Contingency: 8%

The resulting internal labor cost is $1,400.00 and direct project expenses are $850.00. After $225.00 of overhead and $198.00 of contingency, total estimated delivery cost is $2,673.00, or approximately $66.83 per internal hour.

Project cost calculator FAQ

What is an internal hourly cost?

It is the internal cost assigned to each hour of delivery work. It may reflect compensation and employment or business costs, but it is not the client-facing billable rate.

What belongs in direct project expenses?

Include subcontractors and costs required specifically for this project, such as materials, stock assets, travel, printing, or special licenses. Do not count the same expense twice.

Why allocate overhead to a project?

An overhead allocation assigns part of shared business costs, such as insurance, accounting, software, and administration, to the project so delivery cost is not understated.

Is contingency the same as profit?

No. Contingency is a cost-planning allowance for reasonable delivery uncertainty. Profit is considered separately when you turn internal cost into a client quote.

Is total delivery cost the amount I should quote?

Not necessarily. Total delivery cost is an internal planning estimate. A client quote may also reflect profit, value, market conditions, scope terms, and applicable taxes.
