About Freelancer Calculator Hub

Freelancer Calculator Hub is a simple online resource designed for freelancers, consultants, and independent professionals who want to estimate pricing with more confidence.

The site was created to make financial planning easier for people who work independently. Instead of relying on guesswork, users can quickly estimate a sustainable hourly rate and understand their income targets.

The first tool focuses on hourly rate estimation, helping users turn income goals, business expenses, and tax expectations into practical pricing decisions.

This project is built for simple use, clear guidance, and long-term growth. It is designed for freelancers who want a fast and useful tool without unnecessary complexity.

For questions or feedback, please contact the site owner through the available contact channel on the website.
