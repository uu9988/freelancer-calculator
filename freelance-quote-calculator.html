Freelance Quote Calculator

A freelance quote calculator helps you estimate the amount to quote for a project based on effort, scope, and business needs.

Introduction

Professional quotes help clients understand the value of your work and allow you to price confidently.

How It Works

You estimate the time, costs, and profit needed to deliver a project and turn that into a clear quote.

Calculation Explanation

Quotes are usually built from labor, material or tool costs, and a margin that reflects business sustainability.

Examples

  • Estimate a quote for a small client project
  • Compare quotes for different scopes of work
  • Include overhead and contingency in the total

FAQ

What should a quote include?

It should include scope, deliverables, pricing assumptions, timeline, and payment terms.

Can this help with proposals?

Yes. A clear quote supports professional client conversations and improves trust.

How to estimate a freelance quote

Confirm scope and deliverables, estimate the work required, add direct costs, and state assumptions about revisions, deadlines, and items that fall outside the quote.

Quote estimate formula

Quote baseline = estimated hours x sustainable rate + direct project costs + contingency. Taxes should be shown separately when local rules require them.

Quote example

A 24-hour assignment at $90 per hour with $150 in direct costs and a $250 contingency produces a quote baseline of $2,560.
