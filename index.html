How to use this hourly rate calculator

Enter your target annual take-home income, work schedule, business expenses, tax rate, and estimated billable time to compute a practical freelance hourly rate.

Calculation formula

Required pre-tax revenue = (take-home income + business expenses) / (1 - tax rate).

Minimum hourly rate = required pre-tax revenue / billable hours.

Recommended hourly rate = minimum hourly rate × 1.15.

Example

For a $60,000 target income, 5 workdays, 8 hours/day, 80% billable time, 20% tax rate, the calculator estimates your effective hourly rate and revenue targets.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I choose my freelance hourly rate?

Start with your target income, add business expenses and taxes, then divide by realistic billable hours to find your baseline rate.

What should I include in business expenses?

Include software, subscriptions, equipment, marketing costs, insurance, and any other expenses required to run your freelance business.

Does this calculator include taxes?

Yes, you can include an estimated tax rate to factor tax obligations into your required pre-tax revenue.
