Freelance Revenue Calculator

A freelance revenue calculator helps you estimate the income your business can generate from client work, hours, and pricing assumptions.

Introduction

Revenue planning allows freelancers to see whether their workload and pricing are enough to meet goals for growth and stability.

How It Works

You estimate the number of projects or hours you plan to work and combine that with your expected rate to forecast revenue.

Calculation Explanation

Revenue estimates are often based on client volume, rate per hour, or package pricing multiplied by expected output.

Examples

  • Estimate revenue from a certain number of billable hours
  • Forecast income from a set of monthly projects
  • Compare revenue options across different pricing structures

FAQ

How is revenue different from profit?

Revenue is the total amount earned before costs and taxes, while profit is what remains after expenses are deducted.

Can this help with planning?

Yes. Revenue estimates help freelancers make better decisions about workload, pricing, and growth.
