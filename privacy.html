Privacy Policy

Introduction

Freelancer Calculator Hub supports independent professionals with practical online tools for planning rates, income, and business expenses.

Why this site exists

These static tools make it easier to explore pricing and profit scenarios quickly without signing up or installing anything.

This website is a static project built with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. No backend, database, or login system is used.

The calculator runs locally in the browser. No personal data is sent to a server unless you choose to use external services such as web analytics.

If you later add analytics or advertising tools, their privacy terms should be disclosed here.

They provide estimates based on your inputs and help you understand planning assumptions, but they are not professional advice.

Can I use these tools for my freelance business?

Yes, they are built to support freelancers with rate planning, income targets, budgeting, and project estimates.
